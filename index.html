Fortren is the working name of Jose Adrianne Hinaut, a graphic designer in Cebu City. The work here is campaign-led: a product poster has to survive being seen for half a second, and an apparel graphic has to survive being worn. Both are the same problem — decide what the eye lands on first, then protect it.

Most of these started as self-set briefs. Pick a brand worth the trouble, invent the campaign it hasn’t run yet, and build the whole set rather than a single hero image, because a campaign that only works once isn’t a campaign.
